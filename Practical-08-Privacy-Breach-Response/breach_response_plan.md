# Privacy Breach Response Plan

## Scenario
An employee accidentally sends a spreadsheet containing student contact details to the wrong recipient.

## Immediate actions
- Confirm what information was disclosed.
- Identify the recipient and affected records.
- Ask for deletion/return where appropriate.
- Restrict further access.
- Preserve relevant logs and evidence.
- Inform the designated privacy/security team.

## Assessment
Determine:
- Number of affected people
- Types of data
- Whether sensitive data is involved
- Likelihood of misuse
- Whether legal/regulatory notification requirements apply

## Recovery
- Correct access permissions.
- Review email/data-loss controls.
- Notify affected parties when required.
- Document the incident.
- Conduct staff training.

## Lessons learned
The organization should improve data classification, recipient verification and least-privilege controls.
